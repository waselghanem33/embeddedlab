Build a breadboard circuit and inspect its behavior

Explore connected breadboard strips, place jumper wires and components, inspect the electrical network, and analyze a current-limited LED circuit.

A deliberately bounded DC model: one source, one LED and one resistor in a series circuit. This is not SPICE, firmware execution, or hardware-accurate pin simulation.

1 · Configure

Voltage and current are illustrative. Real microcontroller drive capability, LED curves and resistor tolerances vary.

2 · Place components

Click two holes to place the selected element. The LED direction follows the order of your clicks. Click an existing wire or component to remove it.

Select the first hole.

Power rail holes are connected horizontally within each rail. In each numbered column, A–E form one strip and F–J form another. The center gap separates the strips.

3 · Interactive breadboard

Click-to-connect works with mouse, touch and keyboard (Tab then Enter). Components are idealized two-terminal elements; breadboard strips and rails have zero modeled resistance.

CapabilityBehavior

GPIOIdeal 5 V or 3.3 V source; actual output drive and pin safety not modeled
Fault detectionOpen path, bypass/short, reversed LED and overcurrent warnings within this topology
