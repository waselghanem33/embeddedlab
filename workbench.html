Build, test, diagnose

Connect a virtual peripheral to an Arduino Uno or ESP32, run an idealized protocol transaction, inspect logic signals, and save your lab report.

This model checks logical connections and timing conventions; it does not simulate analog electrical behavior, voltage damage, pull-up sizing or compiled firmware.
